# createTraining

## Goal

Record a training request for the authenticated company and persist it in DynamoDB.

## Input

- documentType: `invoice` | `creditNote` | `bill` | `transaction` | `debitNote`
- fromDate: calendar date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format
- toDate: calendar date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format
- idCompany: injected by the authorizer (`organization.legacyId`)
- idUser: injected by the authorizer (`user.legacyId`)

## Validations

- documentType must be one of the allowed values
- fromDate and toDate must be calendar dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` format
- fromDate must be before or equal to toDate
- idCompany and idUser are required

## Output

- Persisted record with `id`, `createdAt`, and `updatedAt` generated by the use case

## Side Effects

- Write to the DynamoDB `training` table, with `pk = COMPANY#<idCompany>` and `sk = TRAINING#<id>`
